# Decision record

For each option, capture its status (proposed, trial, accepted, rejected, or historical), actual input scope, parameters, success criterion, effect on retained observations, limitations, and the evidence location. Report material changes under both reasonable choices if those changes affect the conclusion; do not conflate a comparison of options with an independent replication.

## Synthetic example: a paired regional description

**Question:** In fictional plants with two recorded regions passing a predeclared quality requirement, what is the average within-plant difference, A minus B? Both regional signals must be recorded and pass the requirement.

| Plant | A | B | Qualification | Paired difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| P01 | 8 | 6 | Use: both pass | 2 |
| P02 | 11 | 7 | Use: both pass | 4 |
| P03 | 5 | — | Unavailable: B missing | — |
| P04 | 30 | 8 | Excluded from this comparison: A failed an earlier quality check | — |

Report **2 of 4 plants** eligible, individual differences **2 and 4**, and a **mean of 3 signal units** across those two pairs. Do not substitute zero for P03, treat regions as independent plants, or let P04 disappear from the retention record. A clearly labeled sensitivity description can show what changes if quality eligibility is relaxed, but it does not retroactively change the main rule or restore a general biological inference. These fabricated values do not justify a p-value, biological generalization, or a new measurement.
