# Spatial Omics Paper Reading

Turn a paper into an evidence-linked reading report. For a focused question, use only the relevant steps without rebuilding an existing report.

1. **Verify source.** Confirm title, DOI or stable identifier, article version, and the materials actually accessible: abstract, main text, figures, supplements, or code. Distinguish a referenced supplement from one you opened. If the full text is unavailable, proceed only with material actually inspected and name the resulting limits. If no usable source is available, request one and stop. If an excerpt is usable but lacks an identifier, label its identity unverified and proceed within the excerpt; do not invent a figure citation, image, or missing method. Keep source papers and any library read-only. Deduplicate paper versions if processing a batch; distinguish a correction from an independent study.
2. **Trace design.** State the scientific question, plant material, conditions, comparison, independent sampling unit, observation unit, and assay. Explain how outputs from one step became inputs to the next. Distinguish the experiment's workflow from the article's narrative; figure order does not establish experimental chronology.
3. **Read figures.** For panels central to the question, identify what the plot displays, the observation unit, the comparison, and the claim it supports. Link the panel to its caption and relevant methods or supplement when available. Inspect the rendered panel when scale bars, color keys, or error bars matter; extracted text alone will not show them reliably.
4. **Capture methods.** Record actual parameters with units, software version where documented, source location, quality checks, and differences across study conditions. Preserve published variants rather than averaging thresholds across papers. Separate observed data, model-derived outputs, author interpretation, and transfer suggestions.
5. **Save report.** Lead with article identity and reading basis, then present the question, workflow, key panels, usable methods, and limitations. Start with plain language and add technical detail as needed. Link to panels, describe them, or draw original schematics rather than reproducing unlicensed figures in redistributable material. State what could not be checked, without filling gaps from an AI summary.

Read [Panel and methods record](references/panel-and-methods-record.md) for deeper figure analysis, cross-paper comparison, or an optional handoff. Preserve reader edits and update only the requested scope when continuing a report. A published method is not an approved plan or evidence that it was applied to someone else's data.
