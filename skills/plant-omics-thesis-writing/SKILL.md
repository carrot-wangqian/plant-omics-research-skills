# Plant Omics Thesis Writing

Produce an evidence-grounded draft that preserves the author's decisions and distinguishes results from interpretation.

1. **Set scope.** Identify the author-selected baseline, target sections, protected elements, and output. A later filename is not enough to displace an author-approved source. Read beyond the target only as needed to check coherence. Default to a separate revision without overwriting the baseline.
2. **Map claims.** For each material claim, identify evidence source, sample and comparison, analysis unit, figure or table, and limits. Distinguish protocol intent, actual records, data outputs, external sources, and author confirmations. If sources conflict, isolate the affected claim and continue independent work.
3. **Draft or revise.** Put why the study was needed in the introduction, what was actually done in methods, what was observed in results, and what it may mean in discussion. Preserve material contradictory results or limitations. Lead with evidence and concrete nouns rather than formulaic assurances. Read [Claims and chapter logic](references/claims-and-chapter-logic.md) for assay-specific evidentiary wording.
4. **Verify references.** Match citations to the exact claims and experimental context they support. Verified metadata does not mean a source supports the claim. Check figures and tables against their actual panels, datasets, scale bars and units. Do not invent technical values or replace functioning citation fields with plain text.
5. **Deliver copy.** Preserve figures, existing citation fields, and approved text outside scope. Check changes against the baseline, cross-references, and actual formatting when editing a document. Report what changed, what remains unresolved, and what could not be verified. For supplied format rules and author revisions, read [Document revision and formatting](references/document-revision-and-formatting.md).

Use the strongest claim that the evidence supports, rather than weakening every sentence. If a necessary fact is missing, flag it specifically and name the source needed to settle it. Only call a draft final when the applicable checks were actually completed.
